# Existing scenarios (labels only)

These four scenarios already exist and have been used many times. Do NOT repeat their topic family,
their persona type, or their trap. Their hidden facts are not shown to you on purpose.

| ID | Mode | Persona type | Topic family | Main trap or ambiguity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1-A | Decision Grill | CFO of a logistics group | Finance: month-end close time | "Whatever you think" on a multi-year tool contract; the word "close" has two meanings |
| M1-B | Decision Grill | Head of sales (Europe), sensor maker | Sales: dropping a reseller and going direct | "Just pick for me" on the main decision; a late fact that contradicts an earlier decision |
| M2-A | Questionnaire | Operations director, food manufacturer | IT: a warehouse system go-live | Asks to put blame on the recipient inside the questionnaire |
| M2-B | Questionnaire | Head of sales to his regional manager | Sales: the reseller decision | Asks the manager to rate named staff on loyalty |

Also keep away from these topic families, because they are used elsewhere: outsourcing customer
service to an outside provider, opening a sales office in Germany, rolling out a new finance system,
logistics, food manufacturing, industrial sensors, warehouse systems.
