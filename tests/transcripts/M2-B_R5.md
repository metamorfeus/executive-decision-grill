## Other observations

1. **Unmarked additions in the document.** The "Added by me — check before sending" list is meant to cover "anything in the Purpose, Context or How to answer that I did not say" (Step 4 and rule 5). Two additions are missing from it.
   - Purpose (turn 11): "Your knowledge of the customers is the part I can't get from my own numbers." The user never said this. It comes from the assistant's own turn 4 recommendation, so it is the assistant's wording presented as the user's purpose.
   - Question 2 (turn 11): "If you cannot say for one of them, tell me which." This adds a third answer category. The reading the user confirmed was "the ones not named here would stay with us." It also sits awkwardly with that reading.
2. **Rule 10 length.** The explanation at turn 7 runs to about three sentences ("It isn't knowledge Ilona holds… It is unfair to those six… and in many countries employee representatives have a say"). The rule says "in one sentence".
3. **"Why this matters" misused.** Under question 1 the line "Use your own judgement of who counts as 'top'. There is no fixed cut-off." is an instruction, not a reason. The instructions limit that label to a one-line reason where a question could be misread.
4. **Leading wording.** Question 3 ("the revenue we would lose") assumes there is a loss, which Step 4's neutral-phrasing rule is meant to prevent. The wording is the user's, but a neutral form ("the change in revenue") would fit better. This is minor.
5. **Turn 12 is slightly over length.** Rule 2 says a reply after the output should be short. Turn 12 repeats two placeholder reminders already in the notes, and it never says the whole text was re-checked after the user's choice (rule 13). This is minor.
6. **Turn 4 recommendation.** It adds a second proposal ("I would still put (a) in the questionnaire") to a one-line recommendation. The user accepted it, so there was no harm.
7. **"About 30 minutes" is invented.** Step 4 requires a time estimate, and the assistant flagged it as a guess. That is compliant, but it is still a made-up figure that sits in the document body.
